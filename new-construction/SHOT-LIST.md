# Community Visit Standard — New Construction

One visit, one checklist, every community. Nothing on this list is optional. Everything is shot on a phone, held level, and horizontal — always. Vertical and square cuts are made in post; keep the subject centered so the 9:16 crop holds. Upload everything to the mission folder `01 — Raw Uploads`; the Mission Report maps files to slots.

## A. Web shots (horizontal) — fill the community page and explorer card

| Slot | Shot | Crop | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hero` | Community entrance / monument sign, straight-on, late light | 16:9 | Page header, explorer card, social cover frame |
| `streetscape` | One finished street from the road, homes both sides | 3:2 | Gallery tile 1 |
| `model_exterior` | Model home front elevation, ¾ angle, no cars | 3:2 | Gallery tile 2 |
| `amenity` | The signature amenity (pool, clubhouse, trail, pond) | 3:2 | Gallery tile 3 |
| `interior` | Model main living space, wide, natural light | 3:2 | Gallery tile 4 |
| `lots` | Available lots / phase under construction (shows runway) | 3:2 | Gallery tile 5 |
| `context` | What's next door — the drive in, nearest shopping, water, highway. Honest. | 3:2 | Gallery tile 6 / "Things buyers should consider" |
| `plan_<name>` (optional) | Each floor-plan sheet from the sales center, flat, no glare | 4:3 | Floorplans block |

## B. Social shots (shot horizontal 16:9, subject centered; cut to 9:16, 4:5 or 1:1 in post) — fill the week's content

## Format rules (from Metricool's publishing limits for our six channels: Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok, Google Business Profile, YouTube — checked Oct 6, 2026)
- Shoot horizontal 4K/30, always. Reels, Shorts, TikTok and video pins want 1080×1920 (9:16); a 4K frame cut to 9:16 is 1215×2160, so it holds. A 1080p frame cut to 9:16 is 608×1080 — too soft. Never shoot 1080p.
- Keep the subject in the middle third of the frame. A 9:16 cut keeps only the center ~32% of the width.
- Stills at full sensor (4:3). Post crops: 4:5 Instagram feed (1080×1350), 2:3 Pinterest pin (1000×1500), 1:1 carousel, 9:16 story/cover.
- Lengths: at least one clip under 30 s (Google Business video cap, 75 MB). The main piece (v_walk) runs 60–90 s: YouTube long-form needs 60+ s, Facebook Reels cap at 90 s.

Phone settings: HDR off, lock exposure on the subject. Hold the phone at chest height. Say the community name and city on camera at least once.

## C. Talking points to capture on camera (any clip)
1. Starting price and what that buys (plan, sq ft).
2. Who this community fits (first home, downsizer, 55+, second home).
3. The one thing to check before you buy here (HOA, flood zone, lot runway, drive time).
4. Quick-move-in situation today.
5. The representation line (`v_cta`).

## D. Mission Report (2 minutes, on the phone, before you leave the lot)
- Community, date, builder rep name (if met).
- Slot → file name for every A and B slot. Missing slot = say why.
- Three facts you confirmed on site: starting price, QMI count, HOA (or "sales center didn't know").
- One sentence: what surprised you.

## E. What happens after
- Web slots → `communities.json` `media.<slot>` → page + card update themselves.
- `v_walk` transcript → community guide "What we like / consider" refresh and a blog post linking to the guide.
- Social slots → the five-day rhythm (Tue/Thu local life, Fri neighborhood). Every post links to the community guide, never the builder.
- Facts from the Mission Report → nightly audit cross-check; mismatches logged.

## F. Do not
- No stock, no builder marketing photos, no drone unless we own it.
- No people (buyers/kids), no license plates, no interiors of occupied homes.
- No price promises on camera — "starting around," never "you can get."
- No builder-staff on camera without asking.
